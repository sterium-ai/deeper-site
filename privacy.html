Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy covers the game Deeper on every platform where it is offered (web, Windows, Linux and Android), and the demo site play.steriumai.dev.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The Android version requests no permissions, including no internet access.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, your best depth on each difficulty and your audio settings are stored only on your own device (in your browser's storage for the web version, or in the app's data folder for the downloaded and Android versions). You can delete them at any time by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it.

The demo site

play.steriumai.dev sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Stores

When you buy or download the game from a store (such as itch.io, Steam or Google Play), that store handles your payment and account under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@steriumai.dev.
