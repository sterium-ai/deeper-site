Title

Deeper

Developer

Sterium AI

Genre

Colony survival, mining, base building

Platforms

Windows, Linux, Android, web (free demo)

Status

In development, 2026. Free browser demo available now.

Description

Lead a band of miners into the earth. Dig through glowing fungal caves, crystal hollows, sunken ruins and a molten core, keep your colony fed, rested and lit against the creatures of the dark, and reach the Heart of the Deep, if you can get past what guards it.

Mark tiles and your colonists dig, build and fight on their own. Eight layers, six kinds of dungeon, twelve creatures, ten pickaxe levels, flowing lava, dynamic light and a dragon at the bottom of the world.
